# Phrase Periodicity — bar-sequence autocorrelation of per-stem energy shape

[← archive index](experiments_archive.md)

**Archived** 2026-09-17 — ran, measured, **passed its own kill condition**,
not promoted. z-normalised per-bar 16-slot autocorrelation recovered the
operator-stated 8-bar bass phrase on `Chimera - Hana` (three stems
independently) and phrase prominence cleanly separated the two known-8-bar
songs (`Chimera - Hana` +0.158, `Hideaway - Kiesza` +0.125) from the rest
(max +0.075, `Armin`); a raw-envelope ablation found no phrase anywhere
(≤ +0.03) — the per-bar z-normalisation was the whole method. Not archived
for a negative result: it classifies block **character** (through-composed /
bar-loop / half-bar), which is not a field the MCP contract projects, and its
own known limit (needs a block ≥ 2 bars) rules out sub-second cues, the
`structural_vs_micro` territory. `experiments/phrase_periodicity/` stays in
the tree as the record; the `3. Phrase Periodicity` debugger lane was
removed.
