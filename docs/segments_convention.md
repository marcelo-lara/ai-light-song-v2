# EDM Section name convention

Here is a comprehensive list of section names you can use to tag your EDM track, grouped by their function in the arrangement. Typical bar lengths are included for 128 BPM House and 140 BPM Dubstep where defined.

## 🛠️ Mixing & DJ Utility Sections

* **Intro** — Stripped-back beginning with basic percussion for DJs to beatmatch.
  * 128 BPM House: 16 or 32 bars (approx. 30–60 seconds)
  * 140 BPM Dubstep: 8 or 16 bars (often features a half-time beat or atmospheric intro)

* **Outro** — Stripped-back ending that fades out or reduces to basic drums for mixing out.
  * 128 BPM House: 16 or 32 bars
  * 140 BPM Dubstep: 8 or 16 bars

## 📈 Tension Building Sections

* **Verse / Main** — Introduces the core musical ideas, pads, or vocals.
  * 128 BPM House: 16 bars
  * 140 BPM Dubstep: 16 bars (often carries the main vocal story)

* **Breakdown / Break** — Drops the drums and bass out completely; focuses on melody, chords, and emotion.
  * 128 BPM House: 8 or 16 bars
  * 140 BPM Dubstep: 16 or 32 bars (Dubstep breakdowns tend to be longer and more cinematic)

* **Pre-Build** — A transition zone between the breakdown and the actual build.
  * 128 BPM House: 4 or 8 bars
  * 140 BPM Dubstep: 4 or 8 bars

* **Build-Up / Build** — Ramps up the energy using snare rolls, risers, and pitch bends.
  * 128 BPM House: 8 or 16 bars (16 bars allows for a long, driving filter sweep)
  * 140 BPM Dubstep: 8 bars (shorter and highly explosive)

* **Fill / Pre-Drop** — The final 1 to 4 beats of silence, a vocal phrase, or a quick drum roll right before the beat hits.
  * 128 BPM House: 1 or 2 bars
  * 140 BPM Dubstep: 1 bar (often just the last 2 or 4 beats of a bar for a quick vocal poke)

## 🔥 High-Energy Peak Sections

* **Drop 1** — The first main high-energy chorus/peak of the track.
  * 128 BPM House: 16 or 32 bars (House grooves roll out longer to establish a hypnotic rhythm)
  * 140 BPM Dubstep: 16 bars (Dubstep relies on rapid variation; 16 bars at 140 BPM is standard double-time length)

* **Drop 2** — The second main peak, often with a slight variation in the bassline or lead.
  * 128 BPM House: 16 or 32 bars
  * 140 BPM Dubstep: 16 bars (frequently alters the synth patches or rhythm pattern from Drop 1)

* **Drop 3 / Extended Drop** — Common in longer club formats or specific genres like Trance.
  * 128 BPM House: 16 bars (rarely used unless it's a long progressive house track)
  * 140 BPM Dubstep: 8 or 16 bars (often acts as a shorter "VIP" or final energetic push)

## 🔄 Transition Sections

* **Bridge** — A short connecting section between a drop and the next breakdown.
  * 128 BPM House: 4 or 8 bars
  * 140 BPM Dubstep: 4 or 8 bars

* **Mid-Intro** — Used in extended club mixes, a transition section after the first drop before the main breakdown.
  * 128 BPM House: 16 bars
  * 140 BPM Dubstep: 8 or 16 bars
